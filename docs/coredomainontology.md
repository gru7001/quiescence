## Core Ontology

### Shared World

The **shared world** is the global state.

It contains the common, public side of the system: the things that exist in the world and the relations among them.

### Vehicle

A **vehicle** is a distinguished part of the shared world that may be driven.

It is the world-embedded thing through which behavior is expressed.

### Driver

A **driver** is the thing that drives a vehicle.

It is the controlling side relative to that vehicle.

## Informal Summary

* **shared world**: common state
* **vehicle**: world-side thing that can be driven
* **driver**: thing that drives a vehicle
